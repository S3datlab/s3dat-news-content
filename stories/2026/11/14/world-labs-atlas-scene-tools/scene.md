# Atlas makes a scene portable
Concept: miniature.
Stake: a convincing invented room and a faithful reconstruction require different checks.
Physical scene: an architectural model workshop; a miniature room is already rotating on a hand-cranked turntable at frame 0.
Cast: no people or characters; the room model, a real camera on a short tabletop track, two removable wall panels and a measuring caliper.
Action: the camera travels around the model while one unfinished wall section slides into its matching recess.
Attention ingredient 1: a satisfying mechanical fit makes the transition from an image-facing facade to an editable physical structure legible.
Attention ingredient 2: the caliper stops at the newly fitted section, making measurement distinct from visual plausibility.
Logo surface: a large blank enamel plaque faces the camera above the turntable, unobstructed for a real World Labs logo added in post.
Banner loop: one shallow camera arc reveals the sliding wall, then returns to the opening angle as the turntable completes its rotation.
Composition: photoreal physical miniature; medium framing; model and blank plaque in the upper half; calm shadowed tabletop in the lower-left for the headline.
Light: warm workshop lamp from upper right, soft daylight fill, concrete wood and plaster textures; no holograms or neon.
Safety: no text, numbers, watermarks or generated logos; this is a conceptual workshop scene, not a claimed product demonstration.
